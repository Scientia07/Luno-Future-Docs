Recommendations by Use Case

School Program Concepts

Best choices: Scrollytelling or Gamma Style - they tell a story and look professional without overwhelming.

Technical Workshops

Best choices: Slidev or Reveal.js - perfect for code examples and technical content.

Marketing to Decision Makers

Best choices: Framer Style or Motion Canvas - premium feel that impresses.

Quick Turnaround

Best choice: Gamma Style - fastest to create while still looking professional.

Offline Distribution

Best choices: Scrollytelling, Reveal.js, or Lottie + HTML - work without internet.

Maximum Engagement

Best choices: Scrollytelling or Framer Style - most interactive and memorable.

Data-Heavy Reports

Best choice: ECharts - interactive charts, drill-downs, and real-time data visualization.

Wow-Factor Presentations

Best choice: Impress.js - 3D spatial transitions that leave lasting impressions.

Premium Scroll Experiences

Best choice: GSAP + Lenis - agency-quality horizontal scrolling and smooth animations.
